# Hi, I'm Helmi Satria 👋

I like finding the problems that keep coming back and building things that make them go away for good.

Product Engineer with 8 years of experience and a frontend background. At INA Digital Edu, my work covers internal products, identity services, QA automation, and shared libraries, including deciding what to improve and maintaining it after launch.

## What I do

My responsibilities include building and maintaining Kiwi, Outline, and Backstage, along with QA automation tools for API and web testing. The work also covers our design system component library, shared tooling library, and identity service, including its auth SDK.

Other work includes project boilerplates and internal platforms that replace repetitive compliance work. I work with different teams to decide what they can share and how it should work.

Reusable components, documentation, proposals, and working examples help teams put these tools to use.

## How I work

I move quickly from a problem to working software, and stay involved after it ships. That means deciding what needs to change and taking responsibility for keeping the product useful and working.

Frontend is my strongest area, but the work goes wherever the product needs it. Sometimes that means a backend change. Sometimes a small script or a better guide is enough.
